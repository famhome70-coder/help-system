🚀Getting Started

Creating a family

  1. Open FamHome and enter your name.
  2. Tap "Create a new family code."
  3. You'll get a short code, like MAPLE-42 — this is how others join.
  4. You become the operator automatically.

Joining a family

  1. Get the family code (or join link) from whoever created it.
  2. Enter your name and the code, then tap Connect.
  3. Came from a join link? The code fills in automatically — just add your name.

Operators can tap "Copy join link" in the header to share an invite instantly.

📍Map & Location

Everyone's location shows live on a real map. Tap "Share my real location" to turn on live GPS — your phone will ask permission.

Every family member's card shows distance from you (e.g. "450 m away"), updating as people move.

You can also drag any pin manually — handy for testing without needing to physically move.

🏠Saved Places & Arrival Alerts

Operators can save places like Home, School, or a friend's house. Tap "+ Add place," then tap the map to drop a pin, name it, and pick a category.

Everyone gets notified automatically the moment someone arrives at or leaves a saved place — no need to check manually.

🔔Reminders & Homework Photos

Operators can send one-tap reminders: 💧 Drink water, 🍎 Eat something, 📚 Do homework.

📷 Homework reminders require photo proof

The child sees a "Homework" card with an Attach photo button — tapping it opens their camera directly. Once submitted, operators see a thumbnail to review.

💬Chat

Group chat — message the whole family at once.

Private chat — tap any family member's name in the chat tab to open a 1-on-1 conversation. Private messages never appear in the group thread.

😴Sleep Tracking

How it works: if FamHome hasn't been opened for a while, that time counts as "asleep." Opening the app and using it for 5+ continuous minutes confirms "awake" and logs the sleep session automatically.

Excellent8+ hours
Good6.5–8 hours
Poor5–6.5 hours
TerribleUnder 5 hours

You can also add a sleep entry manually (bedtime + wake time) for nights the automatic system missed.

Note: this estimates sleep from app activity, not real sleep sensors — a helpful pattern-tracker, not medical data.

📚Quizzes & Streaks

Operators assign a quiz by subject (Math, English, Science, Basic Knowledge) and grade (1–9) to any child.

Kids take the quiz anytime from their own Quizzes tab — 3 multiple-choice questions, with math questions freshly generated each time.

Passing on a new day builds a 🔥 streak — missing a day resets it back to 1.

🎨Personalizing Your Background

Tap "🎨 Background" in the header to pick your own look — 4 preset moods (Warm, Meadow, Sky, Blush) or your own custom color.

This is personal to you only — it doesn't change what anyone else in your family sees.

🚪Leaving & Switching Accounts

Log out — signs you out on this device but keeps your spot in the family. Use this to switch who's logged in.

Leave family — permanently removes you from the group. You'll need a code to join again.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

Can two people have the same name in one family?+

Try to avoid it — your name is used to identify you when reconnecting, so a duplicate could get confused with someone else's.

Why isn't my location updating?+

Make sure you tapped "Share my real location" and allowed location permission when your browser asked. If the app/tab is closed, location stops updating.

Can FamHome lock my child's other apps or games?+

Not yet — that requires a native phone app with special permissions. FamHome currently manages access within itself only, like the homework-photo requirement.

Is my family's data private?+

It's visible to everyone who has your family code — so only share the code or join link with people you trust.

What if I lose the family code?+

Ask any current operator to share the "Copy join link" with you again.
